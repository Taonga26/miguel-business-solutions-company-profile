MIGUEL BUSINESS SOLUTIONS — COMPANY PROFILE WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: page structure and company profile content
- style.css: responsive visual design and MBS colour palette
- script.js: mobile navigation, current year and scroll reveal
- assets/mbs-logo.jpg: cropped MBS logo from the supplied letterhead

To preview:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. For local development, you can use VS Code Live Server.

The site uses the navy, bright blue, white and grey palette from the supplied MBS letterhead.
Company facts and contact information are taken from the supplied company profile. The contact
details shown are the company-profile details (Plot 45D2, Kabwata; miguelbusiness1@gmail.com).
The letterhead has different contact details, so those have not been substituted into the profile.
